This example demonstrates how the three tiers work with a $250,000 ARV.

Strategy Calculation Result

Seller Carry Example:

Seller Financing Example:
$187,500 purchase price → $0 down → seller carries $187,500. At 6% interest with 30-year amortization, the payment is approximately $1,124/month with a 5-year balloon.
